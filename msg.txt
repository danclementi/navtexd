HPSDR: channels share one radio (several receivers per stream)

Channels that use the same radio address now share one session: the
radio is asked for one receiver per channel, each is tuned to its own
channel, and the interleaved samples are split into one stream per
channel. All must use the same sample rate. Tested against the fake
radio with three receivers; not yet on real hardware.
