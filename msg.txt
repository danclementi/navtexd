Second decoding channel, two-line channel header, decoder lock logging

Second channel
- Decode two channels at once, each with its own input (audio, I/Q, radio,
  file), frequency, DDC, receiver, decoder and segmentation settings.
  Channel 1 keeps the existing top-level config groups; channel 2 uses the
  same groups under "second" (plus second.enabled). Fixed at two channels.
- Runner manages one chain per channel; settings changes restart only the
  channel that needs it. Resync, polarity, UoS, autotune, carrier, clips
  and the scope take a channel (?ch=0|1).
- Events, records, hello and /api/state carry the channel (rcv, omitted = 0).
  Records store rcv; the record comment is the operator comment plus the
  channel comment. Log lines get a "[Name] " prefix when channel 2 is on.
  Report/log filter by channel.
- New per-channel channel.name and channel.comment (live-editable); names
  default to "Channel 1" / "Channel 2".
- Per-channel input format reported in run state (e.g. "audio 48 kHz,
  LO 517.000 kHz").

UI
- Header redesign: Comment field removed; Save clip moved next to the sun
  times; two small spectra side by side (each with Auto, LOCK, carrier,
  LOG/LIN); one line per channel under them with name chip (selects the
  active channel), frequency, input, comment, input format, RESYNC,
  SYNC/DATA LEDs, UoS and polarity; "+ Add second channel" chip.
- Options: Input/Decoder/Channels pages replaced by "Channel 1" and
  "Channel 2" tabs (named after the channel names), each holding that
  channel's channel/input/receiver/decoder/segment/ddc groups.

Decoder diagnostics
- Framing lock/unlock events are logged with duration, word count and
  invalid-word count: "unlock (lost signal) after 40.4 s, 587 words,
  185 invalid"; relocks are labelled "(re-lock after fade)".
- "Carrier tracking" log lines are suppressed unless the carrier moved at
  least 10 Hz from the last logged value.
