Spectrum view center: zoom, pan, and slice follows the carrier

channel.view_offset_hz centers the spectrum slice and the receiver's
frequency window away from the channel frequency (restarts the channel).
Administrators can pan beyond the slice or drag the carrier marker to an
edge; a manual carrier more than 1000 Hz from the center moves it too.
Everyone can zoom (wheel) and pan inside the slice; double-click resets.
Real-audio slices are centered on the view as well.
