HPSDR: 63-byte discovery request and clearer discovery errors; TODO updates

Send the standard 63-byte Metis/Hermes discovery request (some radios and
emulators, such as the UberSDR bridge, need the full length). When no radio
answers, the error now says what the address did: replied with something
that is not a discovery reply (with the first bytes), refused the UDP port,
or stayed silent. Test for both.
